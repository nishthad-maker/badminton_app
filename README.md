# Hustler — Train Smart. Hustle Harder.

## What is Hustler?

Hustler is a free mobile app that gives badminton players structured, personalized training — right where they live. Over 300 million people play badminton worldwide, but most don't have access to professional coaching. Hustler puts a personal coach in every player's pocket — no expensive gym, no private trainer, just your phone and the drive to improve.

## The Problem

Young badminton players who want to get better face three barriers:

- **No structure** — without a coach, there's no training plan, no progression, no one telling you what to work on next
- **No connection** — players train alone in their local parks and community centers with no way to find other players, share knowledge, or discover tournaments nearby
- **No accountability** — there's no way to track progress, stay consistent, or know if you're actually improving

## The Solution

Hustler solves all three by combining personalized training, progress tracking, and community features in one app.

**Personalized Training** — Exercises organized across four categories (Strength, Footwork, Endurance, Recovery), each with video demonstrations and step-by-step instructions. A recommendation engine analyzes your training across eight data points and suggests what to focus on next.

**Progress Tracking** — Log every session in seconds. Track your streak, weekly goals, and category balance. See exactly where you're strong and where you need work.

**Community** — A moderated forum where players share tips, ask questions, and post local tournaments with photos. Players find each other, compete together, and build a real sports community in their neighborhood.

**Calendar & Event Planning** — Plan training days, schedule tournaments, and mark rest days — all color-coded. Upcoming events show on the home screen so you never miss what's next.

## How It Strengthens Communities

Hustler is built around the question: *How can we use technology to strengthen communities and improve the lives of people where they live?*

**SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities & Communities** — The Local Tournaments board, community forum, and shared event planning connect isolated players into a real local sports community. Training becomes a neighborhood activity, not a solo effort.

**SDG 16 — Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions** — The community forum is governed by content moderation, user reporting, and automatic hiding of harmful content. It's a safe, inclusive, and accountable digital space built on fairness.

## Features

- Recommendation engine with personalized training suggestions
- Video-guided exercises across 4 categories
- Session logging with emoji mood tracking
- Streak tracking and weekly goal progress
- Community forum with 5 topics (Local Tournaments, General Discussion, Training Tips, Recovery & Wellness, Equipment)
- Image uploads on forum posts
- Content moderation with blocked-word filtering, user reporting, and auto-hiding
- Calendar with color-coded events (Tournament, Training, Rest Day, Custom)
- Quick-add events from the home screen
- Full monthly calendar view with event management
- Voice notes on exercises for personal coaching reflections
- Custom workout creation with Cloudinary video upload
- Profile editing and onboarding flow

## Built With

- React Native & Expo SDK 56
- TypeScript
- Supabase (Auth, PostgreSQL, Row-Level Security, Storage)
- Cloudinary (video and image hosting)
- Expo Router (file-based navigation)

## Who Is It For?

Badminton players aged 8–20 — casual or competitive — who have big dreams but no professional coach. Hustler gives them the structure, tracking, and community that professional athletes have, completely free.

## Why I Built It

When I played badminton, I trained completely alone. I had no coach, no plan, and no idea if other players in my area were looking for the same thing. I built Hustler so no athlete has to feel that isolation — so every player can find structure, track their growth, and connect with a community of players right where they live.
